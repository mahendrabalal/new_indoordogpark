# Indoor Dog Park Editorial & Content Guidelines

## 1. Core Philosophy: Human-First & Utility-Driven
Our goal is to be the single most reliable, practical, and honest directory and resource for indoor dog parks, canine safety, and urban dog parenting.

Every article must deliver direct value within the first two paragraphs (Bottom-Line-Up-Front).

---

## 2. Prohibited AI & Meta-SEO Patterns (Never Include)

1. **Never mention "E-E-A-T", "Google Guidelines", or "Search Algorithm" in article copy.**
   - *Bad:* "To satisfy Google's E-E-A-T standards, we reviewed..."
   - *Good:* "We evaluated each park based on flooring safety, staff certifications, and cleanliness."
2. **Never use AI throat-clearing openings:**
   - *Bad:* "In today's fast-paced world, pet parents are increasingly looking for..."
   - *Bad:* "[City] is widely recognized as one of the most vibrant..."
   - *Bad:* "Whether you are wading through mountain streams or..."
   - *Good:* Get straight to the recommendation: "If you need an air-conditioned play space in Irvine during 90°F heatwaves, here are the top 4 vetted facilities."
3. **Never stuff exact-match anchor links:**
   - Maximum 1–2 internal links per 400 words.
   - Anchor text must flow naturally within sentences (e.g. "learn how to [check asphalt temperatures](#)" rather than repetitive keyword stuffing like "our [summer dog heat safety guidelines](#) and [veterinary weather safety calculator](#)").
4. **No unverifiable expert claims:**
   - Do not claim a veterinarian reviewed the post unless an actual named veterinarian with verifiable credentials provided notes.
   - Speak from the honest perspective of dog owners, facility visitors, and pet community researchers.

---

## 3. Article Structure Standard (City Guides)

1. **Title:** Clear, direct city and topic name.
2. **Quick Answer / Top Picks (BLUF):**
   - 2–3 sentences summarizing the climate challenge (summer heat, rain, winter ice) and naming the top 2–3 standout indoor venues.
3. **Comparison Table:**
   - Clean, mobile-friendly summary of venue names, areas, key features, pricing/membership, and webcam availability.
4. **Venue Spotlights:**
   - Real address, phone number, website link.
   - Practical details: Surface type (rubber, K9 turf, epoxy), dog size separation rules, evaluation requirements, and parking tips.
5. **Practical FAQ:**
   - Real owner questions (vaccination requirements, drop-in vs. monthly membership, aggressive dog policies).

---

## 4. Article Structure Standard (Canine Safety & Health)

1. **The Problem & Immediate Action:**
   - What is the hazard (e.g. hot pavement, foxtails, kennel cough), and what should the owner do right now?
2. **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
   - Clear bullet points with practical thresholds (e.g. "The 7-Second Hand Test for pavement").
3. **Indoor Alternatives:**
   - How indoor parks and home enrichment solve the issue safely.
