## What to Look for in a Long Island Indoor Dog Park

When evaluating an indoor facility in Suffolk County, you should prioritize health and safety above all else. Here are the core factors that expert veterinarians and behavioral specialists recommend:

### 1. Advanced Air Filtration
Because dogs are running, panting, and kicking up dust, a high-quality HVAC system is non-negotiable. The best facilities use UV air scrubbers and frequent air cycling to prevent the spread of airborne illnesses like kennel cough.

### 2. Specialized Flooring
Concrete floors can be incredibly damaging to a dog's paw pads and joints, especially during high-impact indoor zoomies. Look for facilities that utilize high-density rubberized flooring, epoxy coatings with grip additives, or specialized indoor turf (like K9Grass) that offers cushioning and easy sanitization.

### 3. Staff-to-Dog Ratios
An indoor park is only as safe as its supervisors. Industry best practices recommend a ratio of one trained human handler for every 10 to 15 dogs. Ensure the staff is trained in reading canine body language to de-escalate rough play before it becomes aggressive. 

### 4. Mandatory Vaccinations and Temperament Tests
Never bring your dog to an indoor facility that does not require proof of vaccinations (Rabies, Distemper/Parvo, and Bordetella) and a behavioral evaluation. These rules exist to protect your dog.
