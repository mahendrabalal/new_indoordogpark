## Why this wording works

| ❌ Old (wrong) | ✅ New (correct) |
|---------------|----------------|
| "You've been featured" | "You're listed" — honest |
| "View Your Premium Listing" | "View your free listing" — no false claims |
| "Upgraded to Featured status" | Badge is just a free trust signal — no conditions |
| Badge = reward for backlink | Badge = free marketing asset for their customers |

## The business model (for reference)

| Tier | How earned | What they get |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| **Standard** | Auto-listed | Page on directory |
| **Verified** | Claim form (free) | Can edit info, Verified tick |
| **Featured** | Paid | Top of city/state pages, highlighted card |
| **Premium** | Higher paid tier | Analytics, priority placement, no competitor ads |

The badge is **separate from tiers** — free to everyone, no conditions.
Parks that display it give you backlinks. Parks that don't, still have a listing.
